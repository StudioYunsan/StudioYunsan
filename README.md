### 🎬 &nbsp;What we are

**Yunsan Studio** is a small, hungry team building a **video editing + creative agency**.
We make videos that look expensive and feel personal: tight edits, clean motion, and stories that hold attention past the first ten seconds.

### ⚡ &nbsp;What we do

- 📽️ **Documentary-style edits**: research-driven storytelling with archival footage, maps and motion graphics
- 🧊 **2D & 3D animation**: characters, explainers, product and concept visuals
- ✂️ **Long-form YouTube editing**: pacing, retention, sound design, captions
- 📱 **Short-form content**: Reels, Shorts and TikToks built from long-form
- 🎨 **Creative direction**: thumbnails, channel branding, visual identity
